Step 1 — Register the Church
Important: This app only works inside Pi Browser — not a regular browser. Open your Pi Network app, tap Pi Browser, and go to titheinpi7220.pinet.com.
1
Tap Register on the home screen
Navigate to titheinpi7220.pinet.com in Pi Browser and tap Register. Fill in all the fields below with the church administrator or pastor present if possible.
Church Name
The official name your congregation uses.
Denomination
Catholic, Baptist, Anglican, Pentecostal, etc. Write Non-Denominational if that applies.
City & Country
Where the church is physically located.
Email
A church email address — the Tithe in Pi admin team will send your onboarding PIN here. Use an address the church administrator checks regularly.
Website
Optional but strongly recommended — makes verification much easier in Step 4.
Description
One sentence about your church — who you serve and what you're about.
2
Submit and wait for your PIN
Tap Submit. Your church appears in the directory immediately as Listed. The Tithe in Pi admin team will send an onboarding PIN to the email you registered with. This is sent manually so allow up to 24 hours — check your inbox and spam folder.
✓ You are now Listed. Your church is visible in the global directory. Check your email for the onboarding PIN to continue.
Step 2 — Set Up a Pi Wallet
Do this while waiting for your PIN. The church needs its own Pi wallet to receive donations. This should be controlled by a church leader or administrator — not a volunteer who might leave.
1
Open Pi Browser and go to wallet.pi
Inside the Pi Network app tap Pi Browser and type wallet.pi. This is the official Pi wallet where all donations will arrive.
2
Create the wallet and save your passphrase
Follow the wallet creation steps. When prompted you will receive a 24-word passphrase.
⚠️ Critical — 24-word passphrase
Write all 24 words in order on paper. Store it somewhere safe — not on a phone, not in email. This is the only way to recover the wallet if access is ever lost. If it is lost the funds cannot be recovered.
3
Copy your wallet address
Once created your wallet shows a long string of letters and numbers — this is your wallet address. It looks like:
GBA2PNDHK4ZZGXYZ7KCXTTYWKX757XARK3...
Copy it — you will need it in the next step. You can find it again anytime by opening wallet.pi and tapping your address.
Step 3 — Complete Onboarding
1
Enter your PIN on the Onboard page
Once you receive your PIN by email, open Pi Browser, go to titheinpi7220.pinet.com, tap Onboard in the navigation, and enter your PIN. This unlocks your church's private onboarding steps. Keep your PIN safe — you will need it every time you return to update your listing.
2
Paste your Pi wallet address
Paste the wallet address you copied in Step 2. Double check every character — Pi donations are irreversible. A mistake here means donations go to the wrong address and cannot be recovered.
3
Complete your church profile
While in the onboarding flow fill in additional details — leader name and title, physical address, charity registration number if available. The more complete your profile the more trust donors place in your listing. These fields appear in the onboarding steps after wallet entry.
✓ You are now Pi-Ready. Pioneers worldwide can find your church and donate directly to your wallet. This is the minimum needed to start receiving Pi donations.
Step 4 — Submit Verification Proof
Why bother? Verified churches receive significantly more donations. Donors trust verified listings. This step takes 5 minutes and makes a real difference. See Guide 4 for full details on all verification levels.
1
Post your wallet address publicly
On your church's official website or Facebook page post a message that includes your Pi wallet address. Something simple like: "Our church now accepts Pi donations. Send Pi to: [your wallet address]." This proves the wallet belongs to your church.
2
Return to your onboarding page with your PIN
Go back to the Onboard page, enter your PIN, and find the verification step. Select where you posted — website, Facebook, or other social media — and paste the link to the post. Tap Submit.
3
Wait for review — usually 2 to 5 days
The Tithe in Pi team independently checks your link and confirms the wallet address matches your listing. Once approved your status upgrades to Verified automatically and you receive an email confirmation.
✓ You are now Verified. Your listing shows a verified badge. Donors can give with full confidence.
