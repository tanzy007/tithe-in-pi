A Guide for Congregants
Your church is registered on Tithe in Pi — a free global directory for Pi-accepting ministries. This guide shows you how to start giving digitally using your Pi wallet.
Step 1 — Get Started with Pi
1

Download & Register

Download the Pi Network app from Google Play, the Apple App Store, or visit minepi.com. Create your account and start mining Pi daily by pressing the lightning button.

📎 Use This Referral Code When You Register
Using this referral code when you sign up helps support our ministry and increases your starting mining rate. Enter it in the referral field during registration.
2

Complete KYC Verification

To send Pi you must verify your identity inside the app. Look for the KYC section in your profile. This is required before donating and can take some time to be approved.

Important: You must be KYC verified to send Pi. If you haven't completed verification yet, start the process now — it can take some time to be approved.
Ready to Donate?
Step 2 — Make Your Tithe
1

Open Pi Browser

Inside the Pi Network app, open the Pi Browser and navigate to your church's listing on Tithe in Pi.

2

Find Your Church

Search the directory for your church by name or city. Tap your church's listing to open it.

3

Send Your Donation

Copy your church's Pi wallet address and send your tithe directly from your Pi wallet. The funds go straight to your church — Tithe in Pi never holds money.

Safe giving: Always verify you have the right wallet address before sending. Donations are direct and irreversible — double check before confirming.
