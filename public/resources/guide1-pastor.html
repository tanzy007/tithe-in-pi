Pioneer Guide 1 of 4
How to Talk to Your Pastor
You want to tithe your Pi to your church. Your pastor has probably never heard of Pi. This guide gives you everything you need to walk into that conversation with confidence — and walk out with a yes.
Before You Go
1
Open the app in Pi Browser
This app only works inside Pi Browser — not a regular browser. Open your Pi Network app, tap Pi Browser, and go to titheinpi7220.pinet.com. Check if your church is already listed. If it is — great, show your pastor. If not — you're about to change that.
2
Know your one sentence
You don't need to explain blockchain. You don't need to explain crypto. You need one sentence that makes sense to anyone.
Your one sentence"Pi Network is a digital currency with 60 million members worldwide, built by Stanford graduates. Many of them are Christian and want to tithe — but most churches aren't set up to receive it yet."
3
Ask for 10 minutes — not a big meeting
Ask for 10 minutes with whoever handles church administration or finances. Low stakes. Low pressure. Just a conversation between a congregation member and their pastor. You don't need to prepare a presentation — the app on your phone is all you need.
The Conversation
Once You're Sitting Down
The opening line — once you're in the meeting"I want to tithe to the church, but in a currency you've probably never heard of. Can you help me?"
Why this works: It's disarming and honest. It puts them in the helper role — they can't say no to a congregation member who wants to give. It naturally leads them to ask "what currency?" which is exactly where you want to go.
When They Ask "What Currency?"
Your answer"It's called Pi. It's a live digital currency currently trading on several live exchanges — I can show you a live price chart right now. 60 million people have it, many of them Christian. There's a free app called Tithe in Pi that connects pioneers like me with churches that accept Pi donations. I'd love to show you — it takes about 10 minutes to get your church listed and costs absolutely nothing."
Show Them the App
1
Open Tithe in Pi on your phone
Open Pi Browser and go to titheinpi7220.pinet.com. Show them the directory. Show them a verified church profile. Show them the donate button. Let the app speak for itself.
2
Paint the picture
"Your church would have a profile here with your name, location, and a donate button. Pioneers worldwide who want to give to a Christian church can find you and send directly to your wallet."
3
Make the ask — keep it small
The ask"Registration is completely free and takes about 10 minutes. The church sets up a Pi wallet, gets listed, and when anyone donates it goes directly to you — the platform never touches the funds. Can I help you get set up right now? I have a step by step guide on my phone."
If They Say Yes
✓
Open Guide 2 and start registering
Go to Guide 2 — How to Register Your Church. You can walk through the whole registration together right there in the meeting. All you need is their church email address and 10 minutes.
Handling Objections
"Is this a scam?"
I'm a member of this congregation — I wouldn't bring something here I didn't trust myself. Tithe in Pi is a free directory. Registration costs nothing. Donations go directly from the donor's wallet to yours — nobody in between. You can remove your listing anytime.
"What is Pi actually worth?"
Pi is a live cryptocurrency trading on several live exchanges right now. Here — I can show you the live price on my phone. Either way it costs the church nothing to be listed and nothing to receive donations.
"We'd need to discuss this with the board."
Completely understood. There's no pressure and no timeline. Registration will always be free. When you're ready I'm happy to come back and answer any questions the board has — or walk you through it myself.
"We don't really use technology like this."
Your congregation members handle everything on their end. The church just needs a Pi wallet address — which takes 5 minutes to set up. After that you don't need to do anything. Donations arrive directly to your wallet.
"What if the value drops to zero?"
Then you've received a gift that cost your church nothing to accept. Any Pi donated to your ministry is real value at today's market price. There's no downside for the church in being listed.
Remember: You are not selling crypto to your pastor. You are a congregation member trying to give. Lead with that every time and stay there.
