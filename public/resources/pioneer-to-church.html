What is Pi Network?
Pi is a digital currency being developed by Stanford graduates. Over 60 million people have joined since 2019, mining Pi on their phones. As Pi moves toward full open trading, many pioneers want to tithe their Pi to their church.

Registering our church on Tithe in Pi would allow:

  • Our congregation to tithe using their Pi wallets
  • Pi-holding Christians worldwide to find and support our ministry
  • Our church to be verified and trusted on a global platform
  • A new stream of digital giving at no cost to the church

Registration is completely free, takes only a few minutes, and the platform never holds or touches any funds — donations go directly wallet to wallet.

I would be happy to assist with the registration process or answer any questions you may have.
