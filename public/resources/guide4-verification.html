How to Get Verified
Verified churches receive more donations. Donors trust verified listings. This guide explains every trust level, what documents you need for each one, and exactly how to submit your proof.
The 5 Trust Levels
L
Listed
Your church is in the directory. Basic information is visible to donors worldwide. No wallet attached yet.
How to reach this: Complete registration. Happens automatically.
P
Pi-Ready
Your church has a Pi wallet address. Donors can now send Pi directly to you. This is the minimum level required to receive any donations.
How to reach this: Add your Pi wallet address in the onboarding flow. Happens automatically.
⏳
Pending Verification
You have submitted proof that the wallet address belongs to your church. The Tithe in Pi team is reviewing your submission.
How to reach this: Submit verification proof — see steps below.
✓
Verified
Your church has been independently verified. Donors can give with full confidence. Verified churches receive significantly more donations than unverified listings.
How to reach this: Approved after submitting verification proof — see below.
★
Highly Verified
The highest trust level. Reserved for churches that provide comprehensive documentation. The gold standard in the directory.
How to reach this: Contact hello.titheinpi@gmail.com and provide all of the following:
— Charity registration number
— Physical church address
— Leader name and title
— Official charity documentation if available
How to Get from Pi-Ready to Verified
The core idea: Post your Pi wallet address publicly on your church's official website or social media. This proves the wallet belongs to your church — not a random individual. The Tithe in Pi team checks the link independently.
1
Post your wallet address publicly
On your church website or official Facebook page post a message that includes your Pi wallet address. Something simple like: "Our church now accepts Pi donations. Send Pi to: [your wallet address]." The post must be on an official church account — not a personal account.
2
Return to your onboarding page with your PIN
Open Pi Browser, go to titheinpi7220.pinet.com, tap Onboard, and enter your PIN. Find the verification step in your onboarding flow. Select where you posted — website, Facebook, or other social media — and paste the direct link to the post. Tap Submit.
3
Wait for review — usually 2 to 5 days
The Tithe in Pi team independently checks your link and confirms the wallet address matches your listing. Once approved your status upgrades to Verified automatically and you receive an email confirmation at the address you registered with.
✓ You are now Verified. Your listing shows a verified badge. Donors can give with full confidence. You are part of the trusted global faith giving network on Pi.
Frequently Asked Questions
Why does verification matter?
Donors are sending real Pi to your church. They want to know it's going to the right place. A verified badge tells them the wallet address has been independently confirmed as belonging to your church.
What if we don't have a website or Facebook?
A WhatsApp group post showing your wallet address may be accepted as alternative proof. Contact hello.titheinpi@gmail.com before submitting and we will confirm what is acceptable for your situation.
I don't have my PIN anymore.
Email hello.titheinpi@gmail.com from the address you registered with and we will reset your PIN.
How long does verification take?
Usually 2 to 5 days. If you haven't heard back after a week please email hello.titheinpi@gmail.com.
Can we update our listing later?
Yes — return to the Onboard page anytime with your PIN to update your wallet address, contact details, or profile information.
Can we remove our listing?
Yes — email hello.titheinpi@gmail.com anytime and we will remove it promptly.
