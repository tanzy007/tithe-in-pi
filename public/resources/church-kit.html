The Steps to Verification
1
Register Your Church
Takes 2 minutes · Gets you to Listed status
Visit Tithe in Pi in the Pi Browser and tap Register. Fill in your church name, denomination, city, country, email, website, and a short description. Submit and your church appears in the directory immediately.
Status after this step: Listed
2
Set Up Your Pi Wallet
Required before receiving donations
Open the Pi Browser and visit wallet.pi to create your ministry's Pi wallet. Write down your 24-word passphrase and store it somewhere safe and secure — this is the only way to recover your wallet if you lose access. Never share it with anyone. A church leader or administrator should control this wallet.
3
Add Your Pi Wallet to Your Listing
Enables donations · Gets you to Pi-Ready
In your church onboarding page, paste your ministry's Pi wallet address. This is the address donors will send Pi to directly. Double check it is correct — donations are irreversible. Once saved your status upgrades automatically.
Status after this step: Pi-Ready
4
Submit Verification Proof
Builds donor trust · Gets you to Verified
Post your Pi wallet address publicly on your church website or official social media page. Then return to your onboarding page and select where you posted. Our team will independently verify the link and upgrade your status within a few days.
Status after this step: Verified
Highly Verified status is awarded to churches that provide additional documentation such as a charity registration number, physical address, and verified leader information. Contact us if you'd like to pursue this.
Frequently Asked Questions
Is there any cost to register?
No. Registration, listing, and verification are completely free. Tithe in Pi takes no commission on donations.
Does Tithe in Pi hold our donations?
Never. All donations go directly from the donor's Pi wallet to your church's Pi wallet. We are a directory, not a payment processor.
What is Pi Network?
Pi is a digital currency developed by Stanford graduates with over 60 million members worldwide. Many pioneers are Christians who want to tithe their Pi to their local church.
Can we remove our listing later?
Yes. Contact us at hello.titheinpi@gmail.com and we will remove your listing promptly.
