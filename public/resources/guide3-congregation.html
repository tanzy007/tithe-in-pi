How to Tell Your Congregation
Your church is listed. Now the people in the pews need to know. This guide gives you everything you need to spread the word — from a Sunday announcement to a WhatsApp message to a one on one conversation.
Your Referral Code — Share This Every Time
1
Find your referral code
Your Pi username is your referral code. Find it in your Pi Network app profile. When congregation members download Pi and enter your username during signup, your mining rate increases and they start with a head start. Include it in every announcement.
📎 Your Referral Code
Your referral code is your Pi username. Share it verbally, write it on a card, or copy it from your Pi profile to paste into messages. You cannot write on this screen — copy your username from the Pi app and include it wherever you share these templates.
Sunday Announcement
Keep it to 60 seconds. Don't explain Pi Network. Don't explain blockchain. Just tell them the church can now receive digital gifts and where to go to give.
Sample Sunday announcement "Quick announcement — our church is now listed on Tithe in Pi, a free global directory for Pioneers who want to give digitally. If you're a Pioneer you can now tithe directly to our church. Open Pi Browser, go to titheinpi7220.pinet.com, search for us, and tap Donate. If you're not a pioneer yet and want to learn more, speak to me after the service. God bless."
